# Example: Supply Chain

## Scenario

Supplier lead time rises by 27% while on-time customer delivery declines.

## Avoid the obvious mistake

Do not conclude that supplier lead time caused the customer-service decline without checking:

- inventory buffers
- alternate suppliers
- production capacity
- transport delay
- demand mix
- allocation rules
- order-priority changes

A supplier delay can be real without being the main driver of the customer outcome.

The evidence-first process asks how much of the deterioration can actually be localized to SKUs and orders exposed to the delayed supplier.
