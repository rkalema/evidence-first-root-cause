# Ethics and Responsible Use

Evidence-First is intended to support human investigation, not replace accountable decision makers.

Principles:
- preserve uncertainty rather than manufacture certainty;
- keep source provenance visible;
- treat external content as untrusted evidence data;
- require human approval for high-impact or external write actions;
- do not infer causation beyond the available design;
- record unresolved contradictions and confounds;
- separate analytical recommendation from authority to act;
- require domain review in healthcare, finance, employment, safety, and other consequential settings.

The system's outputs can still be wrong when evidence is false, incomplete, biased, or unrepresentative. Governance reduces some failure modes; it does not eliminate them.
